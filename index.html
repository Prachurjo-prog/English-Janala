Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I start learning English on this website?
You can start by exploring our beginner lessons, interactive exercises, and quizzes. We also offer structured courses to guide you step by step.
2. Can I use the platform on my smartphone?
Yes, most features are accessible via mobile browsers or our dedicated app, though certain interactive tasks are better suited for a larger screen.
3. How can I improve my speaking and pronunciation?
We offer live conversation workshops and video resources where you can listen to native speakers and practice identifying key language patterns.
4. Do you provide certificates?
Yes, upon successful completion of a course or level, you will receive a digital certificate or "Digital Badge" that you can add to your CV or LinkedIn profile.
5. Can I change my account's native language?
Yes, you can update your native language in your profile settings to ensure you receive system communications in your preferred language.
